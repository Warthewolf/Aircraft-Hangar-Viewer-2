    Sources. Flight model, loadout and graph stats: DT_PlayerParameter (one table per aircraft, 31 level entries each). Role, base cost and flags: DT_LiveAircraft. Slots: DT_LivePartsSlotSettings. Parts: DT_LiveParts. Missile numbers: DT_WeaponSettings. Airframe features: DT_FlightEnginePartsAnimationConfig.

    How parts are applied. A part changes a value here only when its stated effect names that value: max, break, buffet, stall and minimum speed, max health, stealth level, standard missile count, and the standard missile and QAAM numbers. Rate effects multiply and offset effects add. Graph stats add the part's graph points. Three mappings are my reading of the names: rotation speed X/Y/Z onto the SpeedRot bands, rotation accel and decel onto AddRotR and DecRotR, and decel rate onto DeceleR. Effects with no matching value in these files (for example afterburner start or high-G speed loss) are listed on the part but change nothing here.

    Axes. X, Y and Z are labelled pitch, yaw and roll. That is inferred: the gravity bands act on X most, Y a little and Z not at all. The files do not name the axes or give units for turn rates.

    Weapons. DT_WeaponSettings holds only three rows: two for the standard missile (named C.STORY and EASY) and one for the QAAM. The files do not say which row online play uses. No other weapon's numbers are in these files, so parts for other weapons show their stated effect only.

    Parts have one version. The parts table holds a single version of each of its 60 parts and no part levels. Every part's cost is 0, so slots are the only limit.

    Names, levels, presets. Aircraft and part names are my reading of file codes and effects. Level entry 0 does not fit the 1 to 30 progression and is likely a separate base entry. The PvP preset is my judgement of priorities, not game data. Mission-only variants and the AV-8B (not in the aircraft list) are left out.

    Not shown. The effect tables (engine and vapour visuals) and the bone tables for body and cockpit animation hold no gameplay numbers.
